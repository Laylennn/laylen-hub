# How to earn your first RM1,000 from the services page

Goal: **3 paying clients in 30 days**, working about 5–8 hours a week around classes.

**Your live page:** https://laylen-studio.jinlimw.workers.dev

## Step 0: Set up (30 minutes)
1. Open `services/script.js` and fill in the top two lines: your **WhatsApp number** (012-345 6789 becomes `60123456789`) and your **email**. Until you do, the page shows a yellow "Setup needed" bar and the WhatsApp buttons do nothing, so no customer message is lost to a wrong number.
2. Put the page online on **Cloudflare Pages or Netlify** (free). Follow `services/DEPLOY.md`; it takes about 10 minutes.
   Host your clients' sites the same way.
3. Add that link to your WhatsApp Business profile, Instagram bio and LinkedIn.
4. Set up DuitNow QR / Touch 'n Go so clients can pay you easily.

## Step 1: Build 2 demo sites (weekend 1)
Clients buy what they can see. Make two **fake but realistic** Starter Pages:
- a home baker (menu, prices, "Order on WhatsApp")
- a tuition centre (subjects, timetable, fees)

Put them under `services/demo-bakery/` and `services/demo-tuition/` and link them in the "Recent work" section.

## Step 2: Find people who need you (every week)
Who to look for: businesses that **sell on Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp but have no website**.
- Your area (near campus / your hometown): kedai, cafés, tuition centres, salons, car wash, home bakers
- Google Maps: search "bakery near me" and look for listings with **no website**
- Facebook groups: local buy-and-sell, local food groups, UTAR student marketplace
- Friends and family who run businesses. These are the easiest first yes.

Keep a simple list (Google Sheet): Name · Contact · Where found · Messaged? · Reply · Status.

## Step 3: Message 10 businesses a week
**English**
> Hi! I'm Laylen, an IT student at UTAR. I noticed [business] doesn't have a website yet. I build simple mobile websites with your menu/prices and a WhatsApp order button, from RM299. See my services and prices: https://laylen-studio.jinlimw.workers.dev Would you like me to make a free preview for you?

**Bahasa Melayu**
> Hai! Saya Laylen, pelajar IT di UTAR. Saya perasan [nama bisnes] belum ada laman web. Saya buat laman web mudah untuk telefon, ada menu/harga dan butang order WhatsApp, dari RM299. Servis dan harga saya: https://laylen-studio.jinlimw.workers.dev Nak saya buatkan preview percuma?

Tip: a **free preview** (a 1-hour mock-up with their name and logo) turns far more "maybe"s into "yes".

## Step 4: Close and deliver
1. Agree on the price in writing (a WhatsApp message is fine): what's included, the date, and 50% deposit.
2. Build it, send the preview link, and do up to 2 rounds of changes.
3. Take the balance, launch, and **ask for a testimonial + referral**: "Know anyone else who needs one? I'll give you RM30 per referral."
4. Offer **monthly care (RM49)**. 10 care clients = RM490/month of recurring income.

## Other things you can also offer (same skills)
- **Excel / Google Sheets clean-up and dashboards** for shops (stock, sales). Ask small businesses: "How do you track your sales?"
- **Fiverr / Upwork gigs**: "I will build a responsive landing page" or "I will create an Excel dashboard". Start cheap ($15–$30) to get reviews.
- **Tutoring juniors** in Excel, intro programming or database subjects you already passed.

## Rules to protect yourself
- Register the domain in the **client's** name, and give them every login at the end.
- Income you earn is taxable: keep a record of every payment and declare it to LHDN (Inland Revenue Board) when you file. When it becomes regular, look into registering with SSM. Ask your faculty or a tax adviser if you're unsure.
- Always take a deposit before starting.
- Never build e-commerce/payment systems you can't support. Recommend Shopee/Shopify links instead.
- Keep the client's login details private and hand them over at the end.
- Your studies come first: cap yourself at 1–2 projects at a time.

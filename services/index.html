FAQ

Common questions

Why so cheap compared to agencies?

I’m a student building my portfolio, with no office or sales team. You get good work at a lower price, and I get real experience.

Do I have to pay for hosting?

Usually not. Simple sites run free on GitHub Pages or Netlify. A custom domain (yourname.com.my) costs about RM50–RM120 a year, paid directly to the domain company.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. I’ll show you how, or you can take monthly care (RM49) and just WhatsApp me the changes.

How do I pay?

DuitNow / online bank transfer or Touch ’n Go eWallet. You pay 50% to start and 50% before launch.

What if I don’t like it?

You see a preview before anything goes live, and revisions are included. If we can’t agree after the first draft, I’ll refund half your deposit.
